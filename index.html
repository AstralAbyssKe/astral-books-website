About the Author

Welcome to my corner of the cosmos! As an author fascinated by astronomy, ancient mythologies, and deep-space opera, I created the Solar Eclipse Saga to combine high-stakes galactic intrigue with deeply human stories of discovery.

Book I is published and available worldwide across all major retailers. Books 2 and 3 of the trilogy are currently actively in writing, with chapter updates and exclusive previews regularly released right here in the Short Stories and Concepts archives.
