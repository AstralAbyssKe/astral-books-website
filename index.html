ASTRAL ABYSS

"Step into the expanse of three tales, three universal constants and three fates intertwined."

In an alternate world, chief marine biologists Anton Baldacci and Saraya Sumar discover a new life-form within the abyssal depths of the Diamantina Trench during the last day of the multi-national ‘Endeavour’ mission.

But the ‘life’ they uncover is beyond natural and is a threat to all life on the planet. Its discovery sets off a chain of events that leads to a global military operation and a galactic conflict.

At the centre of everything, Anton is faced with a life-altering dilemma: to lose all he holds dear, or save them at the cost of himself—as powers from beyond the Xzeverin cluster seek to annihilate an uncontrollable human being that ascended on a tiny blue dot.

DIRECTIVE 01

Interagency Contingency Directive - The Mandala Protocol

The Mandala Protocol, enacted on the 17th of June 1964, is an interagency intelligence directive, implemented by a joint taskforce comprising of the CIA, DTC, FBI and the MP. It is the only directive with a level 9 clearance, with only cleared representatives being privy to its directives. The President of the U.P.A (Level 7 Clearance) and every elected members in service(Level 4 Clearance) have no access.

ABOUT THE AUTHOR

Nelson Mwova is a Kenyan science fiction author and geospatial professional with a career in the space and humanitarian sectors. Nelson has been writing since 2018, but it was until 2024 that Nelson began his formal writing journey with the writing of his first book, Astral Abyss, a project sparked by his father’s stories and their shared lifelong fascination with the cosmos. Since then, his portfolio has expanded to include 'The Mandala Protocol' and the upcoming 'Astral Knight' ; narratives that remain connected yet distinct in nature. Nelson explores science fiction by blending plausible scientific concepts with vivid imagination and deeply emotional storytelling that define his passions and ideation of the alternate worlds and their characters. When not penning stories, he is an avid gamer and swimmer.

Book 1, Astral Abyss, is published and available worldwide. Books 2 and 3 of the trilogy are currently actively in writing, with writing updates and teasers released right here and on Substack.

TERMINOLOGY & LORE

UNIVERSE GLOSSARY

The Consortium of Planets

Faction

A massive, cross-galactic order comprised of civilizations that have achieved a “Planetary level” classification. The Consortium acts as a unifying body for functional societies across the stars, aiming to connect with all intelligent civilization and grow from it. Yet, its true mission is not that direct.

Genesis

Technology

A rare and ancient form of living metal resource originally discovered on the planet Burian. Possesses inherent reactive properties that allow for structural malleability and techno-organic amino acid synergies, if its energy barriers are surpassed.

Tristaram

Entity

A singular universal powerhouse and high-ranking member of Legion defined by their unparalleled mastery of life sciences and genetics across galaxy sectors and eras.

Keiber 17

Location

A massive planetary outpost and administrative hub engineered around the compressed core of a captured gas giant at the volatile critical edge of the Xzeverim Cluster.

Pathfinder

Operative

An elite operative in official service of the Consortium of Planets bound by the Creed—an ancient, immutable soul contract that remains binding until death.

The Mandala Protocol

Protocol

An intragovernmental UPA secret protocol whose primary directive is the absolute safeguarding of the global paradigm against unexplained anomalous events or artifacts.

Well Drive

Technology

A spatial-temporal propulsion system that functions by inducing a gravitational singularity within a localized warp bubble, creating a time-dilation effect where internal time slows relative to the universe resulting in faster space travel for beings like Pathfinders.

Legion

Order

An obscure universal order that holds absolute leverage over the primary galactic sectors, operating through a doctrine of absolute control via absolute absence.

PCD (Personal Confidential Device)

Equipment

A digital, standard-military-issue communications device locked with personal biometrics of the officer operating it to relay orders and reports.

UPA (United Provinces of the Americas)

Geopolitical

A massive geopolitical collective composed of sovereign nations reorganized into administrative provinces and governed as one. The core of the UPA is anchored by the North American triad—the USA, Canada, and Mexico. Through extensive expansion and colonial consolidation, the UPA's reach now encompasses all former states in South America and the entirety of Australia, designating them as provincial territories. Under this unified banner, the UPA operates as a singular, dominant force in terrestrial and planetary affairs, acting as one of the four primary stakeholders of the World Council, a member of the Consortium of Planets.
