Astral Abyss

In an alternate world, chief marine biologists Anton Baldacci and Saraya Sumar discover a new life-form within the abyssal depths of the Diamantina Trench during the last day of the multi-national ‘Endeavour’ mission.

But the ‘life’ they uncover is beyond natural and is a threat to all life on the planet. Its discovery sets off a chain of events that leads to a global military operation and a galactic conflict.

At the centre of everything, Anton is faced with a life-altering dilemma: to lose all he holds dear, or save them at the cost of himself—as powers from beyond the Xzeverin cluster seek to annihilate an uncontrollable and powerful human being that ascended on a tiny, pale blue dot.

Astral Knight

Building on the concepts of the first book, a new tale set in the distant Pylis Galaxy on the Planet L'karim, where one of the first civilizations of the galaxies is forming. The tale follows the story of Auron Skye, a noble son to a Lord Bearer of the House Skye and his journey in learning the mathematical arts of divining constellations and stellar essence in hopes of one day joining the Knights Order. Born with a rare condition that limits his speech and hearing, he must wade through these unique challenges, in a world not built for him. For becoming a Knight and succeeding the legacy of his father is his life's purpose.

Yet, it becomes apparent that this is but one side of the story.

For on the neighbouring planet Burian, is the seeds of a new civilization that broke off from L’karim, 13 T'pyberium Cycles before. They are a people forged and hardened by the brutal planet they now inhabit, choosing to abandon their afflicted bodies for mechanical ones. Machined bodies they call Mebolavs, in a process called Conversion. This world and its people is explored through the eyes of Semile’ny Kiliptrion, a non-converted daughter of Fra‘Gram Kiliptrion III, Arbiter of Ultraport 1 and Aspera City.

As these two worlds collide during the negotiations for the T'pyberium Accords, the protagonists find each other as pawns in a chess match dictated before the rise of their own civilizations. For within the Chronomantic Scriptures of L’karim, a Mathematical divination of a possible future was revealed from the very foundations of this world. A possible threat to the cosmic order of the Legionnaires, an ancient order that has worked to develop this and many other civilizations across the Galaxy. A threat made true when the Child of Promise seizes their destiny. One they call Prima Nocturae.

Terminology & Lore

Glossary

Comprehensive definitions of organizations, technologies, and terms across the Astral Universe.

The Consortium of Planets

Faction

A massive, cross-galactic order comprised of civilizations that have achieved a “Planetary level” classification. The Consortium acts as a unifying body for functional societies across the stars, aiming to consolidate all intelligent life into a single, cohesive network. The foundation of this order is attributed to the Envoys—ancient Pathfinders in the service of the Legion who predate most known biological life. Under their guidance, the Consortium aggressively expands its galactic reach and facilitates trans-galactic travel through the distribution of highly advanced technology. While it presents as a beacon of progress and unity, its underlying connection to the Legion and the ancient Pathfinders suggests a more calculated purpose behind the consolidation of intelligent species.

Genesis

Technology

A rare and ancient form of living metal resource originally discovered on the planet Burian, according to the historical records of TRISTARAM. Unlike static ores, Genesis possesses inherent reactive properties that allow for structural malleability. The material's potential was significantly expanded when a member of the Legion harnessed the finite supply, enhancing both its raw energy output and its reconstructive capabilities. The evolution of the substance reached a breakthrough when a Pathfinder successfully integrated the metal with biological matter through complex amino-acid synergies, creating a true techno-organic hybrid. As the technology matured, its later iterations achieved significantly higher frequencies of vibratory energy capacitance. These advanced, high-resonance forms are classified as Picto-Geneses, representing the pinnacle of the material’s ability to store and channel immense energetic loads.

Tristaram

Entity

A single-being universal powerhouse and a high-ranking member of Legion. Tristaram is defined by an unparalleled mastery of life sciences, genetics, and the complexities of the vast life era. His influence extends across galactical regions through the deployment of hundreds of Pathfinders, who serve as the instruments of his directives and ensure the continuation of his research. Despite his immense reach, Tristaram remains an enigma; no living being has ever gazed upon his true form. He operates through a layer of extreme isolation, utilizing his Pathfinders as the front-line enforcement of his will. These subordinates are notorious throughout the galaxy as being both exceptionally brutal and entirely expendable, reflecting Tristaram's cold, clinical focus on his work above the value of individual lives.

Keiber 17

Location

A massive planetary outpost and administrative hub for the Consortium of Planets. Originally engineered by the Nimulic Explorers, the station is an artificial shell constructed entirely around the compressed core of a captured gas giant. Strategically positioned at the volatile ‘critical edge’ of the Xzeverim Cluster, it serves as a melting pot for numerous galactic powers. Despite the diverse and often conflicting interests of its inhabitants, Keiber 17’s absolute sovereignty remains unchallenged, maintained by the sheer power and tactical superiority of its owners. Pathfinders.

Pathfinder

Operative

An elite operative in the official service of the Consortium of Planets, a trans-galactical organization dedicated to the preservation of universal life. The title is granted to any sentient being deemed worthy of the Creed—an ancient, immutable soul contract that remains binding until the moment of death. While nominally serving the Consortium’s mission, Pathfinders operate with a degree of autonomy that eludes total central control, as many are understood to be ultimately beholden to a primordial, older force that predates the Consortium’s own mandate. Legion.

The Mandala Protocol

Protocol

An intragovernmental UPA secret protocol whose primary directive is the absolute safeguarding of the global paradigm against unexplained events, anomalous biologicals, extra-terrestrial artifacts, or incidents capable of triggering global catastrophe.

Well Drive

Technology

A spatial-temporal propulsion system that functions by inducing a gravitational singularity within a localized warp bubble. The drive operates when a toroidal mass spins sinusoidally around the singularity’s event horizon, creating a profound time-dilation effect where internal time slows relative to the surrounding universe. While the ship maintains a constant sub-light speed, this temporal slowing allows it to traverse vast interstellar distances in negligible external time. Structural integrity and internal gravity are maintained via gravitational shielding, which isolates the crew from the singularity’s immense tidal forces. Visually, the manoeuvre is characterized as ‘falling through space,’ where the vessel appears to drop downward through a gravitational map and emerge at a distant coordinate, effectively 'falling' across the stars.

Legion

Order

An obscure universal order that holds absolute leverage over the primary galactic sectors of the known universe. Unlike the overt administrative sectors of the Consortium of Planets, Legion operates through a doctrine of absolute control via absolute absence. They possess influence that transcends borders, civilizations, planetary alliances and even the Ages.

PCD (Personal Confidential Device)

Equipment

A digital, SMI (standard-military-issue) communications device locked with personal biometrics of the officer operating it. It serves as an instrument to relay orders and reports amongst operatives from senior command. These are linked in operation to certain missions, vessels of deployment or bases, ensuring data cannot leave its designated target officer or assigned proximity.

UPA (United Provinces of the Americas)

Geopolitical

A massive geopolitical collective composed of sovereign nations reorganized into administrative provinces. The core of the UPA is anchored by the North American triad—the USA, Canada, and Mexico. Through extensive expansion and colonial consolidation, the UPA's reach now encompasses all former states in South America and the entirety of Australia, designating them as provincial territories. Under this unified banner, the UPA operates as a singular, dominant force in terrestrial and planetary affairs, oacting as one of the four primary stakeholder of the World Council, a member of the Consortium of Planets.
